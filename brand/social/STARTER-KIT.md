# Social media starter kit: StoneSyz and Panzo

Prepared 2026-10-22. Images are in this folder. Character limits marked "about" are
from memory: check each platform's current limit before pasting.

**Plan (agreed direction):** Panzo is the active brand (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn,
X @thepanzoapp, plus YouTube). StoneSyz is light: an active LinkedIn page, and the name
`@stonesyz` reserved everywhere else with logo, bio and link, no regular posting.

**Before creating the StoneSyz LinkedIn page:** decide whether StoneSyz may be linked to
your personal LinkedIn (owner rule: your profile carries no Panzo name, link or pitch
because you work at Simplify Home). A company page needs a personal admin; check what
LinkedIn shows publicly about page admins.

## 4. Panzo on YouTube (worth starting)

Follow Panzo's voice guide (Nexus `panzo/product/social-voice-and-content-guide.md`):
the "Site Buddy" voice, no prices (point to thepanzo.com/pricing), no client data or faces,
and **only live features**: check each one against `panzo/CURRENT.md` before filming.
Use the demo company data, as in the website screenshots.

**Channel:** `@thepanzoapp` (created 2026-10-22), using Panzo's own logo and colours (not the StoneSyz ones).

**First ten videos** (short, 30 to 90 seconds, phone screen recordings):
1. A day on site with Panzo Mobile: open My Day, see what needs you today
2. Send a daily site report with work done, manpower and photos
3. Raise a snag with photos or a short video from the camera
4. Update a task with photos: Still Working, Send For Review or Postpone
5. Raise a service ticket with its type and photos
6. Claim an expense with a photo of the receipt
7. From lead to deal: the sales pipeline in Panzo Studio
8. Track every project's stage, snags and site reports in one list
9. What the owner sees: the management dashboard in two minutes
10. Panzo People (UAE and Singapore): attendance and leave on the same login

**Format tips:** a vertical (Shorts) version for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts,
plus a horizontal one for the channel; captions on every video (most are watched muted);
one task per video; end with "Book a demo: thepanzo.com/contact".
